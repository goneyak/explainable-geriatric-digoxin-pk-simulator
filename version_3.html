Residual error: proportional, σ² = 0.04 (~20 % CV, log-domain).

Bayesian MAP: coordinate-descent grid search on ηCL (and ηV1 if ≥ 2 TDM points). Prior: log-normal IIV. Likelihood: proportional error.

Monte Carlo: 500 virtual patients with IIV on CL, V1, V2, Q. PI = 5th–95th percentile.

DDI Education: Amiodarone (CL×0.75), Verapamil (CL×0.70), Quinidine (CL×0.50) — simplified P-gp-inhibition models.

Validation status: Internal consistency only. No external dataset evaluation. Prediction-error metrics are shown when TDM data are entered.

Intended use: Educational / portfolio demonstration of MIPD concepts. NOT for clinical prescribing.
